## Top sights in Ohrid

| Sight | Area | Time | Entry |
|:---|:---|:---|---:|
| Church of St. John at Kaneo | Kaneo | 45 min | €2 |
| Samuel's Fortress | Upper town | 1 h | €2 |
| Church of St. Sophia | Old town | 40 min | €2 |
| Plaošnik | Old town | 40 min | €2 |
| Ancient Theatre | Old town | 20 min | Free |
| Bay of Bones | Gradište, 16 km south | 1 h | €3 |
| Monastery of St. Naum | 29 km south | Half a day | €2 |
| Galičica National Park | Between Ohrid and Prespa | Half a day | Free |

Most sights are a short walk apart in the old town. **St. Naum** and **Galičica** make good half-day trips.
