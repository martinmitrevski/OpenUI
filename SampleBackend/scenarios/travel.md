## 3 Days in Ohrid

Ohrid sits on the shore of **Lake Ohrid**, one of the oldest lakes in Europe, and its old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Each day keeps to one part of the lake, so you can do it all on foot and by local bus.

**At a glance:** budget **€340** per person · ~7 km walking per day · around **19°C** in October

### Day 1 · Old town & Kaneo
1. **Old Bazaar & the Upper Gate** — coffee on the Čaršija, then walk up through the old town.
2. **Church of St. Sophia** — 11th-century frescoes; go before the tour groups arrive.
3. **Samuel's Fortress & the Ancient Theatre** — walk the walls, then head down to the Hellenistic theatre.
4. **Sunset at Kaneo** — Plaošnik, then the cliff path to St. John at Kaneo.

### Day 2 · The south shore
- Bus toward **St. Naum** from the bus station.
- **Bay of Bones** — a reconstructed Bronze Age village on the water (about €3).
- **Monastery of St. Naum** — peacocks, frescoes and a rowing boat through the springs.

### Day 3 · Lake & mountains
1. **A morning on the lake** — kayak or take a taxi boat from the harbour.
2. **Galičica panorama road** — views over Lake Ohrid and Lake Prespa at once.
3. **Dinner in the old town** — try tavče gravče and a glass of local Vranec.
