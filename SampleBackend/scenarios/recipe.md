## Spaghetti Carbonara

*Silky and Roman-style — 20 minutes, no cream. Serves 2.*

### Ingredients
- 200 g spaghetti
- 100 g guanciale (or pancetta)
- 3 egg yolks + 1 whole egg
- 50 g Pecorino Romano, grated
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper

### Steps
1. **Crisp the guanciale** in a cold pan over medium heat until golden (about 8 minutes). Keep the fat.
2. **Boil the pasta** in well-salted water, 1 minute short of al dente. *Save a mug of pasta water.*
3. **Make the sauce:** whisk the yolks, egg, most of the pecorino and plenty of pepper.
4. **Combine off the heat:** toss the pasta with the guanciale, take the pan off the heat, then add the egg mixture with splashes of pasta water.
5. **Serve immediately** with the remaining pecorino.

> The secret is **tempering the eggs** off the heat so the sauce turns glossy instead of scrambled.

**Nutrition:** ~640 kcal per serving.
