## St. Naum Day Trip

St. Naum is **29 km south** of Ohrid, near the Albanian border. Go early: the springs are calmest in the morning.

1. **09:00 · Bus from Ohrid** — about 45 minutes from the bus station.
2. **10:00 · Monastery of St. Naum** — the 10th-century church and the peacocks.
3. **11:00 · Boat through the springs** — a rowing boat through the springs that feed the lake.
4. **13:00 · Lunch by the water** — restaurants line the shore.
5. **15:00 · Bay of Bones** — the Bronze Age village on the water at Gradište.

**Estimated cost:** about **€17** per person (bus, monastery, boat and museum).
