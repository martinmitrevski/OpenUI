## Hotels in Ohrid under €70

| Hotel | Area | €/night | Rating |
|:---|:---|---:|---:|
| Čaršija Hostel | Old Bazaar | 19 | 4.4 |
| Kaneo Lakeview Rooms | Kaneo | 54 | 4.8 |

- **Best view:** Kaneo Lakeview Rooms — balconies over the lake below St. John at Kaneo.
- **Most character:** Varoš Stone House — a restored 19th-century house next to St. Sophia.
- **Cheapest:** Čaršija Hostel — rooftop terrace a minute from the bazaar.

Raise the budget to **€100** and you also get the *Promenade Boutique Hotel* and the *Naum Springs Lodge* near the monastery.
