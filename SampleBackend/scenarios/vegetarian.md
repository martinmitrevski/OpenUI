## Shakshuka

A one-pan vegetarian dish, ready in **25 minutes**. Serves 2.

### Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- 400 g crushed tomatoes
- 1 red pepper, 1 onion
- 1 tsp cumin
- 60 g feta

### Steps
1. Soften the onion and pepper in olive oil, add cumin, then the tomatoes. Simmer 10 minutes.
2. Make small wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook until the whites set.
3. Crumble over the feta and add fresh herbs. Serve with crusty bread.
